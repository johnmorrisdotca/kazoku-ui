The view controls · The parts over a view · The reading page

Colour and type

Dark first. One warm colour marks what you can do; one cool colour marks where you can go. Numbers, dates, times and units are always in Plex Mono.

Icon button

A real button, 44 by 44 px, an 18 px icon. Clear at rest, filled on hover and when on. Every one has an accessible name.

Control bar

The same on every view, in this order. A view that cannot do one leaves it out. Press the clock to pick any date and time.

Without Your place and Free flight:

Scale switch

Five steps, always in this order. A panel at the left on a desk, a row of pills on a phone.

Object card

One layout for a star, a planet, a moon, a galaxy, a black hole or a rocket. Every number links to its source; a number worked out for the time shown is never blank.

Layers panel

Real checkboxes, in two columns. It also lists the data door's outside sources, each with its credit and licence, so one can be switched off.

Segmented switch

For the language and for the sky culture. The chosen segment is filled; each is a button with aria-pressed.

Buttons

One filled button a screen, for the main thing to do. Everything else is outlined or an icon.
